Enter your Vercel token to see all your projects:



🔑 How to Get Your Vercel Token:

  1. Go to vercel.com/account/tokens
  2. Click "Create Token"
  3. Give it a name (e.g., "Auto Deploy")
  4. Set scope to your account/team
  5. Copy the generated token
  6. Paste it above to see your projects
